# Parody Everything - Market Cream Strategy

## The Core Insight

You're not selling "AI parody generation." You're selling **viral content creation on demand**.

Every parody has the potential to:
- Get shared thousands of times
- Generate earned media coverage
- Create social buzz
- Drive traffic (with your backlinks)

**This is a viral machine. Price it like one.**

---

## Customer Segments (Highest to Lowest Value)

### Tier 1: Agencies & PR Firms ($500-2,000/parody)
- Create viral campaigns for Fortune 500 clients
- Budget is client money, not their own
- Need impressive deliverables for pitches
- **They bill clients $10K+ for "viral content strategy"**

### Tier 2: Brands & Companies ($200-500/parody)
- Self-deprecating marketing (like Wendy's Twitter)
- Event/launch marketing stunts
- Competitor shade (risky, high reward)
- Internal culture/team building

### Tier 3: Influencers & Creators ($50-150/parody)
- Need content that stands out
- Parodies = guaranteed engagement
- Can monetize through sponsorships
- **1 viral parody = thousands of followers**

### Tier 4: Casual Users ($10-25/parody)
- Just for fun
- Share with friends
- One-time novelty use

## Phase 1: Enterprise Launch (Now - Month 6)

### Pricing Structure

| Tier | Price | Parodies | Duration | Features |
|------|-------|----------|----------|----------|
| **Agency** | $499/month | 10/month | Forever | White-label, API, priority support, no backlinks |
| **Business** | $199/month | 5/month | 6 months | Small backlink, custom domains |
| **Creator** | $49/month | 3/month | 3 months | Medium backlink |

### NO FREE TIER YET

Why? Free users:
- Devalue the product
- Create support burden
- Don't convert well when starting expensive
- You can always add free later, can't remove it

**Instead: 7-day money-back guarantee.** Feels risk-free, but they pay.

### Launch Actions

1. **Cold outreach to 50 agencies** - "We built the viral content machine your clients need"
2. **LinkedIn content** - Show parody examples, tag marketing influencers
3. **Product Hunt launch** - But with paid tiers only
4. **Create parodies of famous brands** - Use as portfolio pieces (Amazon, Uber, etc.)

## Pricing Psychology Tactics

### 1. Anchor High
Show enterprise pricing first ($499/mo), then everything else feels cheap.

### 2. Per-Parody vs Subscription
Offer both:
- Subscription = commitment, recurring revenue
- Per-parody = $99/each for one-off agency needs

### 3. Urgency Through Expiration
Parodies expire unless upgraded. This is BRILLIANT - keep it.
- Free: 48 hours
- Paid: Based on tier
- Enterprise: Never

### 4. Usage-Based Upside
Add: "If your parody gets 100K+ views, we take 20% of any sponsorship revenue OR you upgrade to Business tier"

### 5. Remove Backlink as Upsell
"Pay $50 to remove backlinks from this parody" - pure margin

## Risk Mitigation

### Legal
- Clear "parody" disclaimers
- Don't parody individuals (only companies/brands)
- Terms of service protecting you

### Brand Backlash
- Some brands won't like being parodied
- Position as "flattering satire"
- Offer takedown for serious issues

### AI Costs
- Claude API isn't cheap at scale
- Build caching for popular URLs
- Consider cheaper models for drafts
